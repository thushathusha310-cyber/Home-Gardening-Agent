Tomato Plant Care Information

Tomato plants need sunlight, water, and nutrients.

If tomato leaves turn yellow, possible reasons are:
- Overwatering
- Lack of nutrients
- Poor soil quality
- Plant diseases

Solutions:
- Check soil moisture before watering.
- Avoid giving too much water.
- Add compost or suitable fertilizer.
- Remove damaged leaves.
- Ensure the plant gets 6 to 8 hours of sunlight.

Tomato plants grow well in well-drained soil.

Common gardening problems:
Yellow leaves can indicate nutrient deficiency or watering problems.
Brown leaves can happen due to diseases or lack of water.
